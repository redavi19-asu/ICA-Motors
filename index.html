Service & ownership

Keep customers after the sale.

The customer relationship should not end when the keys are handed over. The ownership side of ICA Motors is designed to keep customers connected to the dealership.

  • Schedule maintenance and repairs
  • See recommended service intervals
  • Request pickup/drop-off service
  • Add detailing or wash service
  • View service history in a customer portal

Standout dealership ideas

Extra experiences

  • Shop by monthly payment: inventory filtering around customer budget.
  • Salesperson pages: customers can book directly with a preferred team member.
  • Vehicle concierge: pickup, delivery, registration, and handoff workflow.
  • Referral rewards: track referrals and dealership credits.
  • Fleet & business sales: dedicated commercial buyer department.
  • EV ownership center: charging help, incentives, setup, and education.
